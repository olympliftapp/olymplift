Olymplift

Privacy Policy

Effective October 2, 2026

Olymplift is a strength training app for iPhone made by Eshan Dhillon ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app handles and why. The short version: your training data stays on your phone, we do not run ads, and we do not track you.

Information stored on your device

The following is saved only on your iPhone and is not sent to us:

  • The name and form of address you enter during setup
  • Your answers to the setup questions, such as your goal, experience, schedule and equipment
  • Your workouts, sets, weights, repetitions and personal records
  • Bodyweight and age, if you choose to enter them
  • Your progress, rank, realms and seals
  • Your app settings and reminder preferences

If you delete the app, this information is deleted with it, unless it is included in a device backup that you control through Apple.

Information handled by others

Apple. Subscriptions are purchased through your Apple Account. Apple processes your payment. We never receive your card details or billing address.

RevenueCat. We use RevenueCat to check whether your subscription is active. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app identifier, your purchase history for Olymplift, and basic technical details such as app version, device type, country and language. It does not receive your name or your training data. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Information you send us

If you send feedback from the app or email us, we receive what you write and your email address if you provide it. We use it only to reply and to improve the app.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, training and rest day reminders are scheduled on your device. They are not sent from our servers. You can turn them off in the app or in iOS Settings at any time.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your information.
  • We do not show ads.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not require an account.
  • We do not connect to Apple Health.

Your choices

  • You can export or erase your data from the Profile tab in the app.
  • Deleting the app removes the data stored on your device.
  • To ask about or delete anything you sent us by email or feedback, contact olympliftapp@outlook.com.

Depending on where you live, you may have legal rights to access, correct or delete your personal information. Contact us and we will help.

Children

Olymplift is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
